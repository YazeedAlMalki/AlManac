When a handoff lists settled decisions and a build order, build under the stated defaults. Do not re-ask, re-plan or open with clarifying questions; flag data-driven issues as they surface, resolve them with the most defensible default, and record the choice in the docs and the summary.

**Why:** On 2026-09-13 Yazeed pushed back on a six-decision planning pass ("stop planning, start building") and asked to be stopped only for the one blocked item (Saudi/Gulf dish data) or anything genuinely not covered.

**How to apply:** Start from the handoff's build order; keep a list of judgement calls made along the way; put any genuine question at the end, together with what is needed from him. Related: [[almanac-doc-locations]].
